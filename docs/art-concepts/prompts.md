## Общая сцена

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. One full-bleed wide landscape 16:9 in-game art direction prototype for Baron Boomhausen, an original comic 2D browser game. Show a side-on gameplay camera just after launch, with simple layered hills near the bottom. A small but clearly readable eccentric 18th-century baron with curled moustache, cocked hat and tailcoat sits upright astride a single round charcoal cannonball, traveling up and right from a low launch pad. A compact stylized puff and a handful of sparks at the pad, plus a clearly separated arc of trajectory dots behind him. Farther in the sky one duck in flight, two distinct colored balloons suspending small prize boxes, and a distant readable round Moon in the upper right. Keep the hero fully visible at about one fifth of frame height, with strong contrast and negative space separating interactable silhouettes from the background. Give the environment and small effects the same art direction as the sprites. Funny, exuberant, juicy color and shapes. One finished illustrative gameplay frame, no typography, no HUD, no labels, no watermark, no montage. No resemblance to any specific existing game.
```

## Варианты

1. [Резиновая анимация](01-rubber-hose.png)

   Rubber-hose theatrical cartoon with emphatic black-plum brush contours, white gloves, sweeping stretchy clouds and round forms. Saturated tomato red, mustard, seafoam and ink with bold flat cel fills. Push physical comedy and squash-and-stretch in the compact blast plume.

2. [Бумажный театр](02-paper-theatre.png)

   Layered cut-paper theater. Crisp hand-cut folds, scallops and ruled edge details, distinct simple silhouettes, tactile colored cardstock with short visible cast shadows. Peaches, turquoise, emerald, acid yellow and saturated vermilion. A whimsical paper diorama flattened for side-on play.

3. [Сказочная гуашь](03-gouache-storybook.png)

   Hand-painted storybook gouache. Opaque matte brushwork, blocked-in foliage and clouds, nuanced pigment texture kept subtle on the clear gameplay layer. Spring green, apricot, lapis blue and cherry red. Warm, mischievous fairytale illustration, with vibrant pigment rather than heavy outlines.

4. [Пиксельная аркада](04-pixel-arcade.png)

   Authentic crisp 16-bit pixel-art arcade scene with an implied low-resolution pixel grid, intentional hard pixel clusters, limited ramp per object, hand-placed highlights, no blur and no subpixel smoothing. Cyan heavens, blue-violet shadows, punchy coral and lemon effects. Clear sprite silhouettes and simple chunky parallax hills.

5. [Ризографический комикс](05-risograph-comic.png)

   Risograph action comic on warm cream newsprint. Oversize geometric speed streaks and carefully controlled halftone shadows. Four punchy spot colors, hot pink, cobalt, orange and a little midnight blue, with tiny deliberate ink misregistration. Open readable cream areas and kinetic print graphics. No lettered sound effects.

6. [Пластилиновый мультфильм](06-clay-stop-motion.png)

   Stop-motion plasticine miniatures captured in a side-on studio diorama. Matte clay, slight finger marks, round clustered clouds and chunky sculpted proportions, with bold daylight and soft short shadows. Saturated mint, poppy red, tangerine and royal blue. Stylize the blast as a sculpted burst of bright clay petals.

7. [Лубочная гравюра](07-folk-print.png)

   Original folk-art woodblock print. Energetic carved edges and stamped surface pattern inside otherwise bold forms, ornamental trees and rolling fields, pictorial flattening and strong color separations. Saturated vermilion, sunflower, deep turquoise and forest green on light cream. Use every ornamental detail sparingly around the key gameplay silhouettes.

8. [Неоновая ночь](08-neon-silhouette.png)

   Neon night arcade with deep midnight-indigo sky, a dark saturated plum landscape, crisp solid turquoise and laser lime rims, magenta prize balloons, tiny star accents and a vivid orange blast. Graphic silhouettes with a few intense glowing cores and very restrained bloom, completely clear at small scale.

9. [Плоская геометрия](09-geometric-flat.png)

   Modern flat geometric cartoon, built from crisp circles, wedges, capsules and jaunty angular cuts, with extremely legible, chunky silhouette design, lively negative space, and simple overlapping color blocks. Vivid sky blue, coral, lemon, deep purple and a bit of mint. Deliberately economical, no texture and no gradients, with an especially punchy geometric puff.

10. [Эмалевые игрушки](10-enamel-toys.png)

   Glossy painted enamel wind-up toy world, presented perfectly side-on. Rounded stamped metal, restrained highlights, warm ivory cloud shapes, playful tiny rivets on a few props, and clean toy-scale contours. Cobalt, bubblegum pink, mandarin and teal with a gleaming lemon blast. Jewel-bright, with large enamel color panels and a light open sky.
